What's happening under the hood?

Step 1: Outbound Push trigger: performance_dip

Vera autonomously initiates contact based on live analytics, formulating a concrete offer (₹999 cleaning) constrained by the merchant's predefined catalog.

Step 2: Intent Classification intent: approval_granted

When the merchant replies "Let's do it", the engine classifies this as a hard commit. It skips chit-chat and instantly hands off to the campaign deployment node.

Step 3: Action Execution action: deploy_campaign

The campaign is pushed live immediately. The merchant receives a confirmation summary. Zero hallucinations, zero intermediate LLM generation required.

Built Different.

Vera 2.0 abandons chatty LLMs for a deterministic, state-driven engine designed purely for merchant conversion.

Auto-Reply Shield

Detects pre-configured WhatsApp auto-replies instantly, logs a suppression key, and halts conversation for 4 hours to avoid looping.

State Management

Instant Intent Handoff

Bypasses conversational pleasantries when a hard commit is detected, immediately routing to the deployment payload generator.

Routing

Service+Price Compulsion

Enforces rigid JSON schema outputs requiring exact service names and prices extracted from the merchant's ground-truth catalog.

Data Integrity

Zero Hallucination Engine

Operates purely on retrieval and deterministic composition logic. Cannot invent prices, services, or policies not in the context store.

Safety Guardrails

Hostile Exit Protocol

Gracefully handles opt-outs, vulgarity, or frustration with professional finality, logging the merchant to a global Do-Not-Disturb register.

Compliance
